⚠️ Not seeing the visual desktop notification?
🌐 Browser: Click the 🔒 padlock icon in the URL bar and select Allow for Notifications.
🪟 Windows: Turn off "Focus Assist" (click the clock in the taskbar). Make sure your browser notifications are enabled in Windows Settings > System > Notifications.
🍎 Mac: Turn off "Focus" (moon icon in the menu bar). Check System Settings > Notifications to ensure your browser is allowed to show alerts.
